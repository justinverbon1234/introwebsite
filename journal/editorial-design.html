Notes on design

Why I Keep Coming Back to Editorial Design

Exploring the relationship between editorial design, typography, fashion, and the way those ideas can shape digital experiences.

Written by Justin Verbon 5 min read

I keep finding myself drawn back to editorial design. There is something about the way magazines combine typography, imagery, spacing, and structure that feels both expressive and incredibly deliberate.

What interests me most is that editorial design does not always try to make everything perfectly balanced or predictable. A layout can feel slightly awkward, oversized, asymmetrical, or even unfinished, while still feeling completely intentional.

That is something I want to explore more in digital design. Websites do not always need to look like a collection of cards, buttons, and perfectly aligned components. They can borrow ideas from magazines, fashion, photography, and print.

Good design does not always need to feel perfect. Sometimes it just needs to feel considered.

This website has become one of the places where I can experiment with that idea. I can treat typography as part of the composition, use negative space more deliberately, and let different sections have their own rhythm while still belonging to the same visual system.

I am still figuring out exactly what my own approach looks like, which is probably the part I enjoy most. The point is not to copy traditional editorial design, but to understand what makes it interesting and see how those ideas can translate into something digital.
