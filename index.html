Who am I?

I am a 25 year old FDND student from Utrecht, exploring the world of art, fashion, and technology.

I like exploring where design, art, fashion, and technology come together. My work is driven by curiosity and experimentation, always trying new ideas, learning as I go, and creating things that feel personal, expressive, and visually considered.
